How TopSummit works.

Fourteen spots. One dollar to start. Half of everything goes down the valley.

01🌐

Plant your URL

Drop your company's link, a one-line pitch, and pick any waypoint on the real Everest route — from Lukla Airstrip to the summit itself.

02💵

Pay the spot price

Open spots cost their base price: $1 at the bottom, $14 at the top. Checkout is handled by Dodo Payments — cards, wallets, and UPI. Prices are tax-inclusive.

03🪓

Cut anyone for $1 more

Any held spot can be taken by paying exactly one dollar more than its holder. They slide a camp down, and the bottom flag gets swept back to Kathmandu.

04❤️

Fund the flood response

50% of every dollar goes to Save the Children's Nepal flood response, plus donations to Nepal's PM Disaster Relief Fund. Getting cut just raises more.

Rules & integrity

Clear rules.
Fair mountain.

No algorithms, no boosts, no hidden mechanics. Position on this mountain is decided by verified payments and nothing else — and every rule fits on one lift ticket.

  • Summary checklist
  • Fourteen spots, base-priced $1 (Lukla) to $14 (the Summit). No free listings.
  • Cutting a held spot always costs its holder's price plus exactly $1.
  • Cut flags slide one waypoint down; the rank-14 flag is swept off the mountain.
  • Cuts are part of the game and are not refunded — they raise more for relief.
  • Flags plant only after the payment webhook verifies — no payment, no flag.
  • 50% of gross claims go to Nepal flood relief; receipts are published here.
